Behind the name

What’s a day boil?

Our name comes from Newfoundland, where a day boil is an afternoon set aside to enjoy good weather, good company, and perhaps a drink outdoors before evening arrives.

The phrase stayed with our founder after a visit—not only for the idea, but for the warmth and generosity of the people he met. It is a small reminder that time is worth using well, and that the best things are often made to be shared.
